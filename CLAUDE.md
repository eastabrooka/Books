## What I like

Books that really resonated: *Radical Candor*, *Doom Guy*, *Every Tool's a
Hammer*, *Range*, *Surrounded by Idiots*, *Algorithms to Live By*, *The Field
Guide to Understanding 'Human Error'*, *Build*.

The pattern: practical lessons from people who've done the job, first-hand
stories from people who built things, and how people and systems really work.

Not for me: fiction (Dungeon Crawler Carl is the exception), dry economic or
science history (I dropped Smil's *Energy and Civilization*), and books written
from the outside by journalists.
